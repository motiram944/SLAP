We just replaced 292 raw prompt tokens with 3 continuous virtual tokens — cutting LLM KV-Cache RAM by 15.2x.

Here is a question almost nobody in AI is asking right now:

Why are we still converting mathematical vectors BACK into paragraphs of English text just to feed them into an LLM?

Think about how traditional RAG works:
1. You turn documents into high-dimensional vectors.
2. You search a vector database.
3. You fetch the raw text chunks (300 to 2,000 words).
4. You stuff all those words into the prompt context.

The consequence?
💥 Your GPU VRAM explodes with bloated KV-Cache memory.
💥 Every single query suffers quadratic attention slowdowns O(N²).
💥 You burn thousands of dollars in token costs for boilerplate context.

We decided to challenge this fundamental assumption.

What if retrieved knowledge NEVER needed to be serialized back into text?
What if the LLM could ingest continuous latent vectors directly?

We ran a head-to-head benchmark on a 20-page technical specification document:

📊 TRADITIONAL VECTOR RAG vs. SLAP (Semantic Latent-Algebraic Protocol):
• Context Window Inflation: 292 tokens ➡️ 19 tokens (93.4% REDUCTION)
• LLM KV-Cache RAM: 96.0 MB ➡️ 6.2 MB (15.2x LESS GPU MEMORY)
• Prefill Attention Operations: 0.78 GFLOPs ➡️ 0.05 GFLOPs (15.5x FEWER FLOPs)
• Storage: 100% Free Tier on Qdrant Cloud (AWS us-east-1) with 24h recycling.

No prompt chunk stuffing. No memory explosion.
