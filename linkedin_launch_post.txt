For the past 2 years, building AI applications meant one thing: 
1. Chunk your documents into text snippets.
2. Store vectors in Pinecone/Chroma.
3. Retrieve top-K chunks and dump 500–2,000 raw text tokens directly into your LLM prompt.

The hidden cost?
💥 KV-Cache memory explosion on your GPU.
💥 Quadratic attention latency O(N²) on every single query.
💥 Skyrocketing token bills and context exhaustion.

Today, I’m excited to unveil SLAP (Semantic Latent-Algebraic Protocol) — an open-source, continuous latent orchestration alternative to traditional RAG.

Instead of stuffing raw text tokens back into your prompt, SLAP projects knowledge directly onto an orthogonal unit hypersphere S^(d-1), compresses residual deltas with >44 dB INT8 quantization, and injects exactly 3 continuous virtual prefix tokens straight into the LLM's hidden space.
